# ReturnTail
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User preference: Next.js App Router, strict TypeScript, React, Tailwind, PostgreSQL on Neon, Vercel, WizardConnect. Implementation choices delegated by the maintainer.

## Users
Small BCH merchants resolving purchase problems; customers seeking a full or partial refund; hackathon judges evaluating the real workflow.

## Product Purpose
A payment problem should not become a trust problem. Connect a verified BCH purchase to a merchant-authorized refund and durable resolution record.

## Positioning
Post-checkout resolution with output-level purchase allocation, atomic refund reservations, explicit wallet authorization, and independent chain evidence.

## Operating Context
BCH BLAZE 2026 Mainnet Track. Deadline supplied by maintainer: November 22, 2026 at 14:00 UTC. Registration and attendance require maintainer confirmation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Locked scope: merchant access, unique-address orders, customer wallet payment, purchase verification, refund requests, merchant decisions, wallet-signed settlements and receipts. BCH refunds are new transactions. No custody, recovery phrases, private spend keys, automatic production transfers, fabricated evidence, or claim that connection proves ownership.

## Brand Commitments
Name ReturnTail; precise, calm merchant-finance voice; original scalable identity; transparent states; free resources; accessible motion.

## Evidence on Hand
Empty initial repository (b6f2fe5), maintainer specification, official documentation. No existing customer metrics, endorsements, transaction proof, credentials, deployed URL, or approved visual identity. Never invent these.

## Product Principles
1. Money movement requires wallet authorization.
2. Chain observations and application assertions are distinguishable.
3. Uncertain outcomes retain reservations and reconcile.
4. One complete resolution journey takes priority over breadth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive desktop/tablet/mobile web, keyboard controls, visible focus, understandable error states, reduced motion, minimum 44px targets.
