## Exact end-to-end owner test

1. Open the HTTPS merchant app; register with a unique password and real store name. In Store & wallet connect the merchant wallet. Confirm the displayed mainnet address, then save the receive public key. Ownership should read unverified.
2. Create a payment request, for example 10,000 sats if within your chosen budget. Save the complete private link. Compare its unique receive address with the merchant wallet's corresponding derived receive index. If the wallet does not recognize the derived address, stop the funded test and report the wallet version.
3. Open the private link in the separate customer browser/profile. Confirm mainnet, amount and recipient. Connect the customer wallet. Prepare a payment. Verify recipient amount, fee, total debit, wallet source paths and change address.
4. Reject the wallet request once; confirm no transaction is marked paid. Cancel the unsigned intent, prepare again, review it, and explicitly authorize the intended BCH payment. Never retry with new inputs after a signed submission becomes uncertain.
5. Check the original transaction in a separate BCH explorer. On ReturnTail use Check payment/Reconcile transactions. First observe unconfirmed, then wait for confirmed. Verify the exact output index and 10,000 sats. A wallet success message alone must not mark it paid.
6. On the customer receipt request a 4,000-sat partial refund to a receiving address you control. In the merchant inbox review reason, destination, amount and original payment. Approve; verify 4,000 reserved and 6,000 remaining. No money has moved yet.
7. Try another request/approval that would bring cumulative approved refunds above 10,000. It must be rejected. Do not use an additional real transfer to test duplicate handling.
8. Prepare the 4,000-sat merchant refund. Verify wallet sources, customer destination, amount, fee, total debit, change, original reference and mainnet. Explicitly sign in the merchant wallet.
9. If broadcast is unknown, leave the reservation in place and reconcile that same txid. Do not construct a replacement. Confirm the new transaction at an independent explorer. Wait for a confirmed receipt. Verify 4,000 settled, 0 reserved and 6,000 remaining.
10. Reload both browsers and restart/redeploy the app. Confirm state persists. Open the public-safe resolution receipt in a logged-out browser. Confirm it omits reason, private link, customer identity and login information.
11. Save original and refund txids, output indices, block hashes/heights, amounts, timestamps, providers, app commit, wallet versions and public URL in the proof ledger. Show actual wallets and real chain results in the final submission recording.
