## Genuine payment and partial refund

1. On the customer page prepare the payment. Compare network, merchant destination, exact satoshis, source addresses, change, fee and total debit with the physical wallet. Disconnect before signing once; no payment may become paid. Reconnect and reject a signing request once. Cancel that unsigned intent, prepare again and explicitly authorize the intended transfer. Never approve an unexpected recipient or an early broadcast.
2. Save the actual payment txid from the persisted attempt. Use Check payment/Reconcile transactions and an independent BCH explorer. Distinguish signed, broadcast, observed unconfirmed and confirmed. Only a confirmed purchase enables a refund. Record the exact output index/amount, block hash/height, confirmations and check time. A wallet success screen alone is insufficient.
3. For a 10,000-sat purchase, request **4,000 sats** back to a standard BCH receiving address controlled by the customer. Use an accurate reason. To demonstrate the server's cumulative approval protection, also submit a **7,000-sat request before approving either**: each is individually within the then-unreserved 10,000, but together they exceed it. No additional transfer is needed for this check.
4. In the merchant inbox approve the 4,000 request. Confirm 4,000 reserved and 6,000 remaining. Approval moves no money. Try approving the 7,000 request: it must fail with `REFUND_LIMIT` (HTTP 409), leaving reservations unchanged. Record the real error and unchanged accounting. Then reject that excessive request to keep the inbox clear. Attempting a 7,000 request only after the first approval would test form/request validation instead of this approval race boundary.
5. Prepare the approved 4,000-sat refund using Paytaca. Select the receive range containing the confirmed purchase. Review original reference, mainnet, merchant sources, customer destination, amount, change, network fee and total debit. Explicitly authorize the **new** BCH transaction in Paytaca. It is not a reversal of the purchase.
6. If the outcome becomes unknown, retain that attempt and reconcile its saved txid. Do not build a replacement, send manually to 'try again', or use new inputs. If recovery is needed, ReturnTail can rebroadcast the identical saved bytes after reconciliation. A timeout is not failure evidence.
7. Independently check the refund txid and recipient output. Wait for confirmed settlement. For the example, verify 4,000 settled, 0 reserved and 6,000 remaining. Check both browsers after reload; records must persist. Open the public resolution receipt while logged out and verify that it omits the private capability, reason, email and session information.
8. Record only actual results in [MAINNET_PROOF](MAINNET_PROOF.md): both txids/outputs, amounts/fee, network, block hashes/heights, check times, primary/witness, wallet versions, app commit, actual rejection and public-safe receipt. Do not publish the customer private link. Only after the confirmed receipt and independent evidence agree may `PUBLIC_PROOF_REFUND_ID` be configured and the site redeployed.
